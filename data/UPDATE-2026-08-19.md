# Snapshot reconciliation: 2026-07-10 to 2026-08-19

This note compares the frozen 2026-07-10 commit datasets with the new live
observation through 2026-08-19. Both miners use the commit's author-local
calendar date. Retrieval occurred during August 19 in Asia/Taipei, so the last
day and the August monthly bucket are partial.

The companion [`UPDATE-2026-08-19.json`](UPDATE-2026-08-19.json) lists every
added, removed, backfilled, and changed hash. It is generated by
`compare_snapshots.py` and intentionally omits names, emails, subjects, and
identity hashes.

The update is a full traversal of newly resolved refs, not an append. A commit
can therefore enter an old month when a moving branch later makes it reachable,
and a previously observed commit can disappear after history changes.

### History drift that changes interpretation

- `triton-ascend` accounts for nearly all backfill. Its current `main` history
  makes 1,082 hashes newly visible with author dates from 2025-08-29 through
  2026-07-10. The old snapshot did not record a source ref SHA, so these rows
  cannot be attributed to activity after July 10. This is a ref-observation
  difference, not a measured burst of work in August.
- One previously observed `triton-ascend` commit is no longer reachable:
  `865691e2e9b656bc58008170207b4108d92e8dd1` (`Move this repo to
  github.com/triton-lang/triton-ascend`, author date 2026-05-18).
- The fixed Heim-window `triton-ascend` total consequently moves from 1,528 to
  2,011, while the narrow Huawei-domain calibration moves only from 301 to 302
  against Heim's approximate 300. The narrow calibration remains the valid
  comparison; the full imported history is not substituted for it.
- One pre-existing `vllm-ascend` no-reply row,
  `bf6450e8792ee4189929651ae7f7b538fa0aedc7`, moved from `unknown` to
  `likely` because the cross-repository same-name heuristic observed new
  confirmed-domain evidence. This illustrates why affiliation tiers are
  recomputed globally.

All other repositories retain the same fixed Heim-window counts. The new
`vllm-ascend` extended window contains 4,569 commits and 1,387,128 cumulative
lines added under the declared rule, versus 3,951 commits and 1,187,044 lines
in the 2026-07-10 snapshot. The line measure includes code, tests, and
documentation.

## Reproducibility and privacy checks

- The GitHub miner was rerun twice against the same eight pinned `HEAD` SHAs;
  all nine output files were byte-identical.
- The GitCode live observation was replayed from its newly recorded three SHAs;
  all three generated files were byte-identical.
- The K3 refresh contains exactly the same 17 unique PR keys as the 2026-08-08
  full snapshot, and every creation timestamp remains inside the fixed window.
- Summary totals and monthly series recompute from the checked-in rows.
- No public row contains author-name or full-email fields. Email-like strings
  in subjects receive best-effort redaction; public hashes and deterministic
  identity hashes remain linkable pseudonyms.

Commit, line, and pull-request volume remain activity measures. They do not
establish correctness, performance, production deployment, employment, or a
causal policy effect.
